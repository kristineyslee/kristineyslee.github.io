**Award:** 2nd Place, Collegiate Rube Goldberg World Championship — Rube Goldberg Institute (Mar. 2025)  
**Team:** Purdue American Society of Mechanical Engineers (ASME) Rube Goldberg Team (Jan. 2025 – May 2025)

A Rube Goldberg machine performs a simple task through a long chain of deliberately over-engineered steps, and every step has to trigger the next one reliably in front of the judges. As a member of the Purdue ASME team, I competed in the Collegiate Rube Goldberg World Championship, where our machine placed 2nd.

**What I did**
* Worked with the whole team through the full process of designing and fabricating the machine's mechanisms
