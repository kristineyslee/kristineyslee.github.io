**Lab:** Microfluids & Soft Matter Laboratory, SNU (Prof. Ho-Young Kim)  
**Period:** Sep. 2025 – Present

Coiled yarn actuators contract when they get wet. By choosing where these actuators go as the fabric is woven, a flat textile can be programmed to change into a specific shape when it meets water.

**What I did**
* Wove water-responsive coiled yarn actuators into textiles in programmed arrangements
* Showed that different actuator arrangements make the same flat textile deform into different shapes when wet
* Applied the textiles to a humidity-responsive filter

This project is ongoing, and detailed results will be shared upon publication.
