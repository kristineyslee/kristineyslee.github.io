**Award:** 3rd Place, SNU ME AI Robot Challenge — Department of Mechanical Engineering, Seoul National University (Jul. 2026)

An AI-driven robot that recognizes target objects with vision and picks and places them autonomously. A vision model was trained on images of fruits and regular polyhedra so that the robot could find the target among different objects.

**What I did**
* Labeled the training images of fruits and polyhedron-shaped objects
* Contributed to training the object recognition model used for pick-and-place
