**Award:** Creative Design Award, ROBOCON in Creative Engineering Design Class — Department of Mechanical Engineering, Seoul National University (Nov. 2022)

As part of the Creative Engineering Design course, teams built remote-controlled robots to compete in ROBOCON: each robot had to grasp designated objects and deliver them into goals. Our robot received the Creative Design Award, and I was later selected as a Korean national representative for [ROBOCON 2023](/portfolio/08-robocon-2023/).

**What I did**
* Led the hardware design and fabrication of our team's robot
