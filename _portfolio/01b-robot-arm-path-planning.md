**Lab:** Laboratory for Advanced Multiscale Manufacturing, Purdue University (Prof. Martin Byung-Guk Jun)

**Program:** Summer Undergraduate Research Fellowship (SURF), May 2025 – Aug. 2025

Developed path planning algorithms for a 6-DOF robotic arm, designing a heuristic cost function for the A* algorithm to generate safe and optimal trajectories.
