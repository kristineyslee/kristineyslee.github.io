**Authors:** Jaemin Eom, Sung Yol Yu, Woongbae Kim, Chunghoon Park, **Kristine Yoonseo Lee**, Kyu-Jin Cho

MOGrip is a robotic gripper designed to grasp multiple objects in a single pick-and-place motion, using translational movements of its fingers.

**My contribution:** Developed an OpenCV-based vision system for object detection, gripper positioning, and grasping-performance evaluation, and analyzed multi-object grasping motions of the human hand using marker-based tracking.
