Spatial Distribution of Flexibility Controls Active Chain Locomotion through Confined Lattices

Kristine Yoonseo Lee, Kwanwoo Kim, Ho-Young Kim*
Poster · IEEE MIT Undergraduate Research Technology Conference (URTC), Cambridge, MA, USA · Oct. 11, 2026

We investigate how the spatial distribution of flexibility along an active chain robot governs its locomotion through confined pillar lattices.

Bi-directional Robotic Integration with Digital Twin for Generalized, Efficient, and Safe Automation in Manufacturing (BRIDGES)

Kristine Yoonseo Lee, Hojun Lee, Han Gyeom Lee, Yuseop Sim, Jiho Lee, Martin Byung-Guk Jun
Poster · Purdue Summer Undergraduate Research Symposium, West Lafayette, IN, USA · Jul. 30, 2025

We present a framework that integrates neural safe-set generation with A* path planning, enabling a 6-DOF robotic arm to move safely and optimally toward autonomous operation in manufacturing. I designed a joint-weighted A* planner and verified the resulting paths in a digital twin and on a real robot arm.

*Corresponding author
