Spatial Distribution of Flexibility Controls Active Chain Locomotion through Confined Lattices

Kristine Yoonseo Lee, Kwanwoo Kim, Ho-Young Kim*
Poster · IEEE MIT Undergraduate Research Technology Conference (URTC), Cambridge, MA, USA · October 11, 2026 *Corresponding author

We investigate how the spatial distribution of flexibility along an active chain robot governs its locomotion through confined pillar lattices.
