MOGrip: Gripper for multiobject grasping in pick-and-place tasks using translational movements of fingers

Jaemin Eom, Sung Yol Yu, Woongbae Kim, Chunghoon Park, Kristine Yoonseo Lee, Kyu-Jin Cho*
Science Robotics, 9(97), eado3939, 2024
*Corresponding author

MOGrip is a robotic gripper designed to grasp multiple objects in a single pick-and-place motion, using translational movements of its fingers.

My contribution: Developed an OpenCV-based vision system for object detection, gripper positioning, and grasping-performance evaluation, and assisted in assembling the gripper prototype.
